— How we work conferences

The three-phase playbook.

Most B2B vendors show up at a conference and hope to network. We start six weeks before the conference opens.

1

Six weeks before: build the base

Network expansion. We find the right people who'll be there. Start conversations without forcing meetings. Many of them don't even know they're going yet. Budgets aren't finalized, tickets are still being bought, and teams haven't locked schedules.

2

Two weeks before: qualify attendance

"Are you going to SiGMA?" Most answers come back as "not sure yet" or "probably not." That's normal. People confirm late. We qualify at scale and keep the warm conversations alive.

3

The last 10 days: conversion window

Replies spike. Calendars open up. Warm conversations turn into meeting slots. By the time your team lands at the conference, the calendar is full. Your team isn't pitching strangers at a booth — they're sitting down with people who already know who you are.

Multi-channel from day one: LinkedIn + conference event app + WhatsApp + Telegram, with one filter — relevant contacts only.
