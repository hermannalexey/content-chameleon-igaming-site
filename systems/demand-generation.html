— How the content actually gets made

Most of what's valuable in your business lives in your founders' heads.

Getting it out is the hardest part of the work.

Each piece of content goes through a four-step process:

1. Content extraction

Your assigned interviewer — your PM, our Chief Editor Ksenia, or one of the three co-founders, depending on your seniority — sits with you for up to 60 minutes a week. Before the session, they've researched your niche, reviewed your team's materials, and written specific questions with example answers attached. When you can't answer something, we triangulate to whoever on your team can. The conversation adapts. So does the next session.

2. Post writing by the copywriter

Your copywriter takes the extracted material and writes the post in your voice. Not a templated tone. Your specific cadence, vocabulary, and conversational moves — captured in the strategic foundation document and refined every month.

3. Internal editing

Every post passes through our Chief Editor before it reaches you. We refine wording, structure, and tone to align with LinkedIn's algorithm and the engagement patterns we've watched work across 40+ iGaming clients.

4. Your approval

The post comes to you with the option to comment, edit, or approve. Nothing publishes without your sign-off.

— Before any post goes live

Your business goal and what success looks like
