— How this agency exists

How this agency exists.

Six years ago, Eugene and Alex ran a recruiting agency. They were nobody. No clients, no name, no track record.

So they did the only thing nobodies could afford to do — they started creating content on LinkedIn. Every week. Consistently. They built the recruiting agency's name from scratch. Clients started coming in.

That's when one of Alex's former employers in iGaming — someone who'd been watching what was happening — got in touch.

"This thing you're doing for yourselves. Can you do it for us?"

Julia joined to help craft that first offer. The three of us started Content Chameleon. His company became our first client.

Six years later, he's still our client. So is the CEO he hired the same month we signed — Lyudmila Milenina at First Sport Media.

That's why we know iGaming. Not because we chose it as a target market. Because our first client was iGaming, our oldest client is iGaming, and most of what we've learned about LinkedIn, we learned by running it for iGaming companies.

— What we stand for

We don't post for the sake of posting.

We started Content Chameleon because every LinkedIn agency we'd seen did the same thing: they templated their clients into sounding identical.

Same hooks. Same humble-brags. Same "agree?" at the end of every post.

That's not ghostwriting. That's plagiarism with extra steps.

A chameleon adapts. We sit with your team every week, pull out the stories you'd never think to share, and write posts that sound like you on your sharpest day. We build your network from the people who actually buy what you sell. We run outreach that reads like it came from a human, because it did.

Six years in, that's still what we sell. The work just got better at it.

— Why iGaming specifically

Why iGaming specifically.

Our first paying client was iGaming. Our longest engagement is iGaming. The conference work we're known for — SiGMA, ICE — is iGaming. Five years in, every one of our best clients was iGaming. The fit was so consistent that staying generalist would have meant ignoring what was actually working.

Eastern European geography turned out to be the asset, not the obstacle. iGaming runs on platforms, games, and traffic — most of which come from teams based in our region. We're closer to your operators, your software vendors, and your affiliate networks than any US or UK agency could be.

We didn't choose iGaming. iGaming chose us, six years ago, and we're still here.
